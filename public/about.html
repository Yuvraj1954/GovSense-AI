About GovSense AI

GovSense AI is an AI-powered civic intelligence platform designed to analyze MPLADS (Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme) data, monitor fund utilization and project execution, identify unusual patterns, and provide evidence-grounded analytical insights.

The platform processes publicly available government datasets from the MoSPI MPLADS Portal (NIC) and applies statistical analysis, machine learning, and natural language generation to produce actionable intelligence for citizens, researchers, and governance stakeholders.

GovSense AI is not an official government service. It is an independent analytical tool built to promote transparency and data-driven civic awareness.

Project Approach

GovSense AI applies a layered analytical pipeline over validated government data to produce evidence-backed intelligence. AI serves as an explanation and intelligence layer over statistical evidence — not as a standalone decision-maker.

1
Government Data Ingestion

Direct ingestion of publicly available MPLADS records from the MoSPI / NIC portal with fingerprint-based change detection.

2
Statistical Analysis

Multi-indicator performance scoring using weighted composite indices, z-score outlier detection, and IQR-based anomaly identification.

3
Performance Intelligence

Composite scoring across fund utilization, completion rate, sanction conversion, execution speed, and anomaly resistance metrics.

4
ML-Based Anomaly Detection

K-means clustering for peer grouping, statistical process control for trend monitoring, and multi-factor risk indexing.

5
Risk Signal Identification

Flags potential irregularity signals, unusual patterns, and analytical attention indicators — not fraud determinations.

6
Project & Member Analytics

Individual work-level analysis, member-level aggregation, state-level benchmarking, and cross-dimensional comparisons.

7
Evidence-Backed Intelligence

Every insight is grounded in statistical evidence. Confidence intervals, sample sizes, and data quality indicators accompany all outputs.

8
AI-Generated Explanations

Natural language narratives generated to explain scores, patterns, and risk signals in human-readable format for civic accessibility.
