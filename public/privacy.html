Govsense AI — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 · Last Updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Govsense AI collects, uses, stores, and protects information. By accessing or using Govsense AI, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described herein.

1

Data Sources & Collection

Govsense AI is a public data analytics platform. All data displayed on this portal is sourced exclusively from publicly available government records.

Primary Data Source

All work records, member profiles, fund allocations, and expenditure data are sourced from the Members of Parliament Local Area Development Scheme (MPLADS) portal operated by the National Informatics Centre (NIC) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI), Government of India.

What we collect:

  • Publicly available MPLADS work records (work name, location, cost, status, dates)
  • Publicly available member profiles (name, party, constituency, house type)
  • Aggregated fund allocation and expenditure summaries
  • State and constituency-level administrative boundaries

What we do NOT collect:

  • Personal identifiable information (PII) beyond public representative profiles
  • Private financial data of any individual
  • Login credentials or authentication tokens (the platform is publicly accessible)
  • Browser cookies for tracking or advertising purposes
  • Location data from users
2

How We Use Data

Data is used solely for the purpose of public interest analytics and transparency reporting. Specifically:

Performance Analytics

Computing performance scores, completion rates, and fund utilization metrics for MPs, MLAs, and States.

Risk Assessment

Identifying statistical anomalies in cost, duration, and execution patterns to flag potential irregularities.

Peer Benchmarking

Enabling meaningful comparisons between comparable entities through cluster-based peer analysis.

Public Accountability

Supporting informed public discourse about MPLADS fund utilization and governance outcomes.

3

Data Storage & Security

  • Hosting:All data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure with industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
  • Access:The platform is publicly accessible. No user accounts or authentication is required. No session data is stored.
  • Retention:Historical data is retained to enable trend analysis. Data is refreshed when new records become available from the NIC portal.
  • Integrity:All data transformations are logged with timestamps. Fingerprint-based change detection ensures data provenance.
4

Third-Party Sharing

Govsense AI does not sell, trade, or share any data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Data may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Public API: Aggregated, anonymized data is available through our public API for research and non-commercial use (see API Documentation).
  • Legal Requirement: If required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Academic Research: Data may be shared with academic institutions for governance research, subject to data use agreements.
5

6

Your Rights

Since Govsense AI processes only publicly available government data and does not collect personal user data, traditional data rights (access, deletion, portability) do not apply in the conventional sense. However, we are committed to:

  • •Transparency: All scoring methodologies and data sources are documented in our Methodology & Audit page.
  • •Corrections: If you believe any data derived from public records is inaccurate, please contact us with the specific record and source of corrected information.
  • •API Access: All data available on the platform is also accessible through our public API.
